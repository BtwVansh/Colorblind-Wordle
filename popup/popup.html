    How the colours are chosen

    Each day's colours come from your local calendar date and one fixed seed built into the extension. Everyone who plays on the same date gets the same three colours, with no server, account or tracking involved.

    Every palette is checked so each pair of colours differs by at least 35 Delta E 2000, and letters use black or white, whichever meets WCAG contrast. The palette stays hidden here until you finish the day's Wordle.
